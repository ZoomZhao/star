## 首页与到账

工具：内置 ImageGen。非透明背景。

Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: a high-fidelity mobile-first product design concept board for a real Chinese family habit and reward app named 星星岛.
Primary request: Design the app COMPLETELY FROM SCRATCH. The user rejected the old interface. Create a beautiful, professionally art-directed, highly usable interface, not a generic dashboard. Ignore existing UI, styles, colors, characters, and assets. This is a working family tool: children finish tasks and hand a physical NFC card to a parent; the parent taps it on an Android phone to award stars instantly, sees a delightful animation, and can later correct the awarded stars. The same physical card awards once per day. Parents also review child submissions and manage task rules.
Composition: One crisp landscape presentation board, approximately 1800x1200, with THREE large flat screen designs side by side. Left is the most prominent mobile parent homepage (390x844), center a mobile NFC success receipt (390x844), right a wider landscape tablet homepage (approximately 800x600) demonstrating a thoughtful responsive layout. Screen contents should be large and readable. Tiny quiet captions beneath screens: “家长首页”, “碰卡到账”, “平板布局”. A muted light lilac board background, no decorative desk props, no device frames, no tilted perspective, no mockup glare. Screens themselves must be convincing shippable UI.
Visual direction: fresh editorial Japanese/Korean family lifestyle app, gentle illustrated charm with disciplined modern product design. Paper-white and very pale lilac surfaces, deep charcoal-indigo typography, confident periwinkle/violet primary actions, buttery sunflower yellow stars, small peach accents. Restrained soft vector illustration and subtle paper texture only in one small focal area. Absolutely no brown-on-beige interface, no green mint palette, no huge pill buttons, no rows of rounded bubble controls, no photorealistic dinosaur pictures, no gradients everywhere. Rounded rectangles 16–24 px, clean spacing, occasional asymmetrical illustration, precise typographic hierarchy and neatly aligned compact rows. Typography modern rounded Chinese sans, sharp and readable, body 13–15px, headings 20–24px, key number 32px. Elegant simple matching outline icons.
LEFT PHONE HOME: Compact status bar and header “吞吞的成长日”, a small child avatar/name selector and date “10月11日 周日”; wallet entry top right with a tiny yellow star and “128”. A compact overview shows “今日获得” “12” plus “已完成 5/8” and “待确认 2”. Main high-frequency action is a distinctive violet action panel, approximately 85px high, with a tasteful tiny illustration of a physical card touching a phone, and text “碰卡发星” and “卡片贴近手机，自动确认”; a quiet small text link “检测卡片” belongs nearby, visually secondary. Section heading “今天的小任务” with unobtrusive horizontal category text tabs “全部  语文  数学  运动”. Four compact task rows with restrained colorful subject symbols, each shows task name, star value, daily progress, and a clear right-aligned action. Exact sample task names: “阅读绘本”, “练习汉字”, “数学练习”, “户外运动”. First two actions “确认完成”, third a light completed state “已完成”, fourth “确认完成”. Do not show huge repeated illustrations or long explanatory sentences inside task rows. Bottom tab bar has four quiet icons with labels “今日”, “待确认”, “心愿”, “更多”. Active tab has a small subtle violet indicator, no individual bubble backgrounds. Ample touch targets, content efficient, no clipping.
CENTER PHONE SUCCESS: Same app visual language. A beautiful contained celebration of stars and a stylized happy star character, softly playful without confetti clutter. Large clear “+3” and “星星到账啦”. Receipt panel below shows “吞吞 · 阅读绘本”, “本次发放 3 星”, “这张卡今天已确认”. Primary violet button “继续碰卡”, secondary text button “修改本次积分”. Make the receipt and accounting outcome unambiguous.
RIGHT TABLET: Match the phone design exactly in colors, typography, icons and spacing. A narrow quiet left navigation rail; useful large center workspace with the same compact overview, NFC action and two columns of compact task rows; a narrow right side section “待确认” showing two small pending task summaries and “查看全部”. The tablet must use the additional width for tasks and review information, not enlarged decoration. No giant empty panels.
Constraints: Render all Chinese text accurately and cleanly. Clear realistic interaction hierarchy. Keep all buttons and content fully inside each screen. No marketing slogans, no giant headings, no gratuitous floating cards or charts. Strong visual quality and careful mobile usability.

## 家长端与管理端

工具：内置 ImageGen。非透明背景。

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: continuation of a production design system for a Chinese family habit/reward app 星星岛.
Use the provided concept board as the visual reference ONLY: preserve its refined pale-lilac/white surfaces, periwinkle-violet actions, deep-indigo typography, buttery yellow stars, compact task rows, rounded rectangles and restrained outlined icon family. This is a new board, not an edit to its contents.
Create one landscape design board with two large flat MOBILE app screens and one TABLET admin screen, all upright, readable and unclipped. Quiet light-lilac background. NO device frames, no tilt, no marketing headline.
Screen 1: PARENT “待确认” screen. Header with back/close icon and title “待确认”, compact “任务 2” “心愿 1” tabs. Two tidy pending task cards with child avatar, name “吞吞”, subject icon book/pencil, task “阅读绘本” or “练习汉字”, submission time, small note. First review expanded to show star quantity chips “1  2  3  4  5”, completion count stepper, a precise total “本次发放 3 星”, clear purple “确认发星” button and secondary “退回”. Lower card compact. Bottom 4-icon nav “今日 / 待确认 / 心愿 / 更多”, review active.
Screen 2: PARENT “更多” screen. Header “更多”. Organized compact settings rows, each with a clear, consistent outline icon on a small pastel rounded-square background, title, concise subtitle and chevron. Sections “任务管理” with “任务规则” (calendar/checklist icon), “制作任务卡” (card/NFC icon), “检测卡片” (scan icon); “偏好与账号” with “主题外观” (palette icon), “提示音” (speaker with toggle), “账号与安全” (shield icon), “退出账号” (logout icon). No empty icon slots, no emoji, no large pill rows. Bottom nav More active.
Screen 3: TABLET ADMIN account management. Same style, usable application layout. Compact left icon navigation “概览 / 家庭 / 账号 / 备份 / 设置”. Header “家庭与账号” and a clearly icon-labeled purple action “添加账号”. Four compact metrics with icons: “家庭 3”, “家长 6”, “小朋友 8”, “已停用 1”. A useful search and filter bar with search, filter and refresh icons. Main account table columns “账号 / 身份 / 所属家庭 / 状态 / 操作”; realistic rows with avatar/name, parent/child role badge, family name, active dot and two icon buttons pencil/edit and key/reset. Names “吞吞”, “妹妹”, “爸爸”, “妈妈”. Right or lower compact “数据备份” section with database/download icon, last backup timestamp, primary “下载完整备份” icon button, and collapsed restrained “恢复备份” disclosure with upload icon. Data protection text concise. Keep at least six account rows visible, no oversized decoration.
Strong requirement: EVERY navigation item and actionable tool has a purpose-appropriate icon. Icons uniform stroke and optical size, NOT mismatched emoji. The app is for adults managing children's routines, warm but efficient and precise. All Chinese text crisp and accurately spelled. No brown/cream/mint styling. No old dinosaur photos or old UI.

## 星星伙伴

工具：内置 ImageGen。透明背景。

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: transparent production illustration for a Chinese family reward app. Use the provided UI concept board only as the visual source. Extract and faithfully recreate ONLY the joyful yellow five-pointed star mascot from the middle phone screen, waving two dark-indigo tiny arms, eyes gently closed with delight, peach blush, tiny smiling open mouth, rounded asymmetrical star points. Match its premium softly textured flat illustration style and beautiful warm yellow. Centered full character with four small isolated yellow twinkle stars nearby. Remove ALL phone UI, background blobs, text, numbers, borders, frames, panels and other objects. Truly transparent background with clean alpha edges. No words, no symbols that resemble letters, no drop shadow. Crisp, high-quality reusable app illustration with generous transparent padding.

## 碰卡插画

工具：内置 ImageGen。透明背景。

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: isolated production UI illustration on a truly transparent background, for the “碰卡发星” action panel in a mobile app.
Create ONLY a small, elegant flat vector-like illustration of a human hand holding a white NFC task card and touching it to a modern dark-indigo smartphone. The card has a simple violet five-point star motif; no text, no letters, no digits. Three very subtle short lavender NFC signal arcs. Use the provided app concept board as the STYLE REFERENCE for its small NFC illustration: contemporary, softly textured, clean geometric forms, lilac, periwinkle, deep indigo, warm peach skin, white card. Device and hand angled slightly to create a clear touching gesture. Composition right-facing, compact, all shapes fully visible, lots of clear alpha around the object. No colored background, no ground, no rectangle panel, no UI controls, no glow, no blur, no cast shadow, no photorealism. Designed to sit on a solid periwinkle action banner at about 130 by 85 logical pixels.
